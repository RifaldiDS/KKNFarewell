BACKGROUND MUSIC
================
Track: Titik Nadir - Kahitna Feat. Monita Tahalea (Karaoke Piano)

Behavior:
- Original website/design is preserved.
- Music begins only when the recipient clicks the envelope or Open your letter.
- Target volume is intentionally soft: 0.16 (16%).
- 2.4 second fade-in to avoid a sudden loud start.
- Small music button allows pause/play.
- Music pauses when the tab is hidden and resumes when the recipient returns (unless manually paused).
- Music fades out when the recipient confirms they have finished reading.
